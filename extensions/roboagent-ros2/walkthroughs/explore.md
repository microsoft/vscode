## Explore the ROS2 graph

The **Package Explorer** (ROS2 activity-bar container) shows every package and, under it, its
nodes, dependencies, interfaces and launch files. Expand a node to see what it **publishes**,
**subscribes** to, and which **services**/**actions** and **parameters** it declares.

Right-click a node to **Run** or **Debug** it, or a package to **Build** just that package.
