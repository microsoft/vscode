## Create your first project

RoboAgent starts where a robotics engineer starts: **what are you building?**

- **High-Level Control** — ROS2, OpenCV or NLP, deployed on host, target or a VM.
- **Low-Level Control** — firmware for an **STM32** or **ESP32** (extensible target database).

The wizard scaffolds a matching starter, records your choice in `.roboagent/project.json`, and
opens the folder so the toolkit and status bar specialize to your project type.
