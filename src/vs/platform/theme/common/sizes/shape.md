## Introduction

Choose roundness by the surface role: controls, inner containers, and floating overlays. Equivalent roles should use equivalent corners.

## Round by surface role

Use the control tier for buttons and inputs, the inner tier for nested containers, and the outer tier for floating surfaces.

## Corner scale

Every registered corner value. Fully rounded is a shape choice, not a larger fixed corner.
