# Elevation

Design guidance and example descriptions. Referenced registrations own the implemented values.

Sources: [SKILL.md](../../../../../.github/skills/design-philosophy/SKILL.md), [SKILL.md](../../../../../.github/skills/ux-theming/SKILL.md).

## Introduction

Layer overlapping surfaces with consistent corners, shadows, and contrast borders.

## Surface hierarchy

Keep the primary surface, supporting layers, and transient overlays distinct.

## Shadow scale

Available workbench shadow levels.

## High contrast

Use explicit borders rather than relying on shadows. High-contrast themes and the no-shadows setting can suppress shadow effects.

## Elevation by role

Illustrative surface tiers: roundness communicates the role in the stack.
