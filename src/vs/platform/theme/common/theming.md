# Theming

Design guidance and example descriptions. Referenced registrations own the implemented values.

Sources: [SKILL.md](../../../../../.github/skills/ux-theming/SKILL.md).

## Introduction

Preserve semantic roles across light, dark and high contrast. Change the values, not the information hierarchy.

## Keep meaning in high contrast

Use solid backgrounds, explicit contrast borders, and visible focus indicators. Do not rely on shadows or color alone.

## Theme comparisons

Compare surface, text, and accent colors, starting with the core light, dark, and high-contrast themes.
